## Engineering a New Route Through the Marine Sulfur Cycle

The ocean contains an enormous diversity of microorganisms and biochemical reactions.

Among these reactions, the metabolism of **dimethylsulfoniopropionate (DMSP)** plays an important role in the marine sulfur cycle.

DMSP is produced by many marine organisms and can be transformed into different sulfur-containing compounds by microorganisms.

One of its important products is **dimethyl sulfide (DMS)**.

---
